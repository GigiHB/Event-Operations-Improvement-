## 1. Executive Summary 
The Event Operations team supports the execution of student programs by coordinating operational tasks across multiple systems, teams and external partners. Daily activities include room assignments, travel manifest preparation, student communications, certificate generation and event documentation.

Current operations rely on multiple disconnected tools, including Microsoft Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, Excel, Dynamics 365, EventHub, Portal Site and other specialized platforms. Information frequently moves manually between systems, creating duplicated work, inconsistent documentation, communication delays and a high dependency on manual processes.

This project proposes an analysis and redesign of selected operational workflows to identify opportunities for process standardization, automation and improved collaboration. The goal is to reduce manual effort, improve data quality and increase visibility of operational activities while maintaining existing business requirements.

## 2. Business Problem
The current operational workflow depends heavily on manual activities performed across multiple disconnected systems.
- Information is frequently exported, reformatted and uploaded between applications, increasing the probability of human error and creating duplicated effort.
- Several operational documents exist in multiple versions, making process standardization difficult and increasing training complexity for new team members.
- Operational visibility is limited because task tracking occurs through shared spreadsheets and manual communication channels instead of centralized workflow management.
- Manual notifications, document generation and data validation consume significant operational time that could be invested in higher-value activities.

## 3. Bussiness Objectives 
The proposed initiative aims to:

- Reduce manual operational tasks.
- Improve consistency across operational processes.
- Increase data accuracy through validation.
- Standardize documentation and workflow execution.
- Improve visibility of operational activities.
- Reduce communication overhead.
- Enable future automation opportunities.
- Support scalability during high-volume program seasons.

## 4. Project Scope

### In Scope 
The initial project focuses on the analysis and redesign of the following operational processes:

- Travel Manifest preparation
- Student room assignment workflow
- Certificate generation process
- Name badge generation
- Operational documentation management
- Internal task tracking
- Manual notification workflows

### Out of Scope

The project will not include:

- Replacement of existing enterprise systems.
- Modifications to Dynamics 365 or EventHub production environments.
- Changes to student registration systems.
- Infrastructure migration.
- Vendor selection.
